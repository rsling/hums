# Editing semantics

Authoritative, together with `CLAUDE.md` (rules, glossary, layers) and `docs/SCHEMA.md` (document model). Items tagged **[Dn]** are defaults whose status is in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

- Clips are **shared by reference** by default. Refcount gate on every mutation of a clip with refcount > 1. Policy comes from a persistent toolbar toggle (`UiState.sharedEditPolicy`), not a modal per edit. Clips with refcount > 1 show a badge (`×3`) in every view. A clip with refcount 1 is simply edited; undo is the safety net.
- **Make unique** acts on the edit context. The view creates one `EditContext` at gesture start, right after `beginNewTransaction`, and passes it to every clip-mutating command of the gesture. The first command that mutates a clip with refcount > 1 under `makeUnique` clones the clip (new UUIDs for the clip and every descendant with an id), redirects exactly the references in the context to the clone, records the clone's id in the context, then applies; every later command in the gesture finds that id and targets the clone. The context dies with the gesture; handing one to a later gesture is a programming error and asserts. A multi-selection of references gets one shared clone. Pool-level editing with no reference selected is "update all" by definition. The original clip stays in the pool (refcount may drop to 0; "remove unused clips" cleans up).
- **The gate in detail** (`model/commands/RefcountGate`, `model/commands/EditContext`). Every change to a clip passes the gate, its name and colour included: renaming a clip through one of its placements under `makeUnique` gives that placement a clone with the new name. A change that changes nothing (the name the clip already has) makes no clone, nor does a command that is refused. The clone stands in the pool directly behind its original and has the original's name. It is made also when the context names every reference of the clip; the original then stays in the pool unused. All references of a context are references of the one clip the gesture changes: a context that names a placement or slot that is not there, an empty slot, or a reference of another clip is refused, under either policy. Once a gesture has cloned, its commands may name the clip by the original's id or by the clone's; both mean the clone, and a third clip is refused. A context used after its gesture is recognised by its undo transaction no longer being the current one; the gate asserts and refuses the command. That is certain once the context has cloned; a context whose gesture had left nothing to undo when it was last used cannot be told from a fresh one, and holds no clone.
- **EventClip content** (`model/commands/EventClipCommands`). A note is added, changed and removed, and so is an event; a clip's loop length and its quantise setting are set. All pass the gate. A command names a note by its position among the clip's notes, and an event by its position among the clip's events, in child order from 0: under `makeUnique` the change lands on the clone, where a node handle of the original names nothing, and the position is the same in both. A new note or event goes behind the clip's other content, so it comes last at its tick; changing one leaves it where it stands. A note may lie at or past the loop length (kept, not played) but must not end past the largest tick an `int64` holds. An event carries only what its kind uses: an `a` or `b` the kind has no use for is 0, and only a `sysex` event has `data`; anything else is refused. Changing the loop length moves and removes no content. Whether a clip is on an AutomationLane (invariant 9) is asked of the clip the change lands on: a clone made through a track's placement takes a note although a lane references the original, and a clone made through a lane's placement takes none.
- **StepClip content** (`model/commands/StepClipCommands`). A lane is added, named, given or relieved of a fixed pitch, moved among the clip's lanes and removed; a cell is given its pitch, on/off, velocity and gate; a cell's CC value for one controller is set (added when the cell has none for it) and removed; the grid's step length, step count and reset are set and a column is skipped or put back. All pass the gate. A command names a lane by its position among the clip's lanes, from 0 at the top, and a cell by its lane and its column: under `makeUnique` the change lands on the clone, whose lanes have ids of their own, and the position is the same in both. A new lane goes below the clip's others with a rest in every column; a clip may have no lane at all. A cell's pitch is kept, and not played, while its lane has a fixed pitch. A gate is stored with six significant digits **[D14]** and is refused when that value is not in `(0, 4]`. The four changes to the grid are loop-length changes (below). Taking a lane's fixed pitch away replaces the Lane node by a copy without the property, so a handle to that lane is stale afterwards; its id stays.
- **Split** defaults to splitting the *placement*: two placements, same clip, the second with an offset. The pool is untouched and playback is unchanged, held notes included. "Crop to new clip" is the explicit command that creates a new clip from a placement's window: notes starting inside the window are kept with their full length, notes starting before it are dropped, events are rebased; `loopLength` = window length.
- **Move** keeps the placement what it is: its id, clip, length and offset stay, along its own track or lane and from one track to another. A placement on an AutomationLane moves only along its lane.
- **Resize** repeats or trims the loop. No time-stretch exists. The command moves the right edge only; whether the left edge gets one too is looked at again after extended use of the first version **[D19]**.
- **No overlap.** A command that would let two placements of one track, or of one lane, share a tick is refused; nothing is pushed aside, trimmed or replaced. One may begin on the tick at which another ends. A placement whose end would lie past the largest tick an `int64` holds is refused too.
- **Loop-length changes** (step count, skip, reset, step length, event-clip loop length) renormalise every referencing placement's offset in the same transaction. A change that would leave no audible column is rejected. How a `stepCount` change treats cells, `skipMask` and `resetAt` is in `docs/SCHEMA.md → Grid`.
- **StepClip → EventClip** is an explicit, one-way conversion, subject to the gate. **EventClip → StepClip** is an explicit lossy import (quantise to the grid, one pitch per cell per lane) with a preview of what is dropped. Conversion creates a new clip; under "update all" every reference of the old clip is redirected to it, under "make unique" only the context's references. The old clip stays in the pool.
- StepClips are editable only in the step sequencer and drum views; EventClips only in the piano roll. Everything created in the step sequencer and drum views is a StepClip.
- **StepClip playback:** off cells are rests (time passes, their CC values are still sent); skipped columns take no time; `resetAt` is the loop end. Cells past `resetAt` and in skipped columns keep their data.
- **Quantisation:** `Clip.quantise` on an EventClip quantises note onsets at playback and export without touching the stored ticks; `applyQuantise` makes it permanent (gate applies). Both are shown in the piano roll and arrangement. The grid is measured from clip tick 0, never from the arrangement; the exact candidate-line and wrap rule is in `docs/SCHEMA.md → ClipPool / Clip`. It is applied once, when the ClipTemplate is built, and every later rendering rule sees only the quantised onset.
- **Session recording** produces a Take: one EventClip per source track that emitted output, captured after session selection, automation merge, mute/solo and live knobs, before physical-port availability. Takes land on new take tracks that copy the source's Player, channel and name, placed at the record start **[D4]**. Clock and transport messages are not part of a take. The take's own playback is never captured.
- **Recording in general** (details written before milestone 6): takes are buffered on the message thread and committed at record stop as one undo transaction, never per incoming event; held notes get note-offs at the stop tick; looped recording overdubs into the same take **[D5]**; a knob recorded into a StepClip stores, per column, the knob's value at that column's start **[D6]**; a knob move always reaches hardware immediately, recording or not; unmatched note-offs are dropped and reported.
- **Knobs:** `Track.Knobs` assigns CC numbers. In clip views a knob move records into the currently playing clip; in the Automation Recorder it records into an AutomationLane placement. With nothing playing a knob only controls hardware.
- **Removing a Player or a Performer** that tracks still use is allowed **[D18]**: the port is removed and every track that used it becomes unassigned (`playerId` or `performerId` emptied) in the same undo transaction, so one undo brings back the port and its tracks' assignments. An unassigned track still renders and exports.
- **Removing a Track** removes its placements, automation lanes, knobs and slots with it, in one undo transaction. The clips they referenced stay in the pool (refcount may drop to 0; "remove unused clips" cleans up), as after make-unique.
- **Slots** hold one clip each, of either kind; putting a clip into an occupied slot replaces the one that was there, which stays in the pool, and no command moves a slot to another row or track: it is cleared there and set here (both to be looked at again after extended use **[D19]**). Slots and scenes are sparse, so there is no last row: every row from 0 on can be named, and one nothing is stored for is empty.
- **Scenes** are rows. Inserting one moves the row that was there and every later one down; removing one empties every track's slot in it, drops its name and moves the later rows up; moving one takes its slots and its name along, and the rows it passes move by one towards the place it left. Each renumbers every track's slots and the scene names in one undo transaction. The clips of a removed row stay in the pool.
- **AutomationLanes** belong to their track: a new lane goes behind the track's others (and so overrides them; a preliminary yes, to be tested in the beta phase of version 1 **[D19]**), a lane is moved only among the lanes of its track, and removing one removes its placements with it in one undo transaction; their clips stay in the pool. A lane takes only an event clip that holds nothing but cc events, an empty one included; any other clip is refused there, and a clip that a lane references is given no other content (`docs/SCHEMA.md`, invariant 9; `model/AutomationClipRule`). A lane's placements keep clear of each other only; what the track itself plays at those ticks is never in the way.
- **PadMap** is input configuration under `Routing`, undoable. Remapping a pad never rewrites clips. An incoming note on the pad Performer matches a pad by `inputPitch`.
