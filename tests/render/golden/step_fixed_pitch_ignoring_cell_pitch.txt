# fixedPitch lane ignoring cell pitch (docs/RENDERING.md -> StepClip;
# docs/SCHEMA.md -> Step clip: Grid).
#
# Four columns of 240 ticks, two lanes, on channel 10:
#
#   lane   fixedPitch   column 0      column 1      column 2      column 3
#   Kick   36           on, pitch 60  off           on, pitch 72  off
#   Hat    42           on            on, pitch 61  on            on
#
# A lane with a fixed pitch plays it in every cell. The pitches the Kick's
# cells hold (60, 72) and the one a Hat cell holds (61) are kept and not
# played; a Hat cell without a stored pitch (default 60) plays 42 as well.
# The Kick has velocity 100, the Hat 70. Every gate is the default 0.5: 120
# ticks.
#
# At a tick where both lanes play, the note-ons are in the order of the
# lanes, Kick first. Their note-offs fall due together 120 ticks later and
# go out in the order in which the notes began.
0 0 10 noteOn 36 100
0 0 10 noteOn 42 70
120 0 10 noteOff 36 64
120 0 10 noteOff 42 64
240 0 10 noteOn 42 70
360 0 10 noteOff 42 64
480 0 10 noteOn 36 100
480 0 10 noteOn 42 70
600 0 10 noteOff 36 64
600 0 10 noteOff 42 64
